# Harman Sadhwani — Immersive 3D Developer Portfolio

A cinematic, high-performance 3D WebGL developer portfolio inspired by **igloo.inc**, engineered for creative developer, full-stack engineer, and AI systems architect **Harman Sadhwani**.

![Harman Sadhwani 3D Portfolio](/Harman.jpg)

## 🏛️ Creative Direction & Aesthetic Identity

- **Futuristic Digital Sanctuary**: Inspired by the spatial elegance and lighting mastery of [igloo.inc](https://igloo.inc/).
- **Pure White & Champagne Gold**: Palette featuring `#FFFBF0` (Ivory), `#D4AF37` (Champagne Gold), and `#07080B` (Deep Obsidian).
- **Crystalline Centerpiece**: Procedural multifaceted crystalline hyper-structures with refractive physical materials (`MeshPhysicalMaterial`), orbiting celestial gyroscope rings, and architectural pillars.
- **Particle Cosmos**: Over 3,600 instanced stardust particles reacting to mouse parallax, scroll acceleration, and orbital gravitational physics.
- **Cinematic Environmental Lighting**: Volumetric exponential fog (`fogExp2`), golden celestial sun key light, sapphire atmospheric rim, and internal crystal luminescence.
- **Continuous Scroll Storytelling**: Smooth camera choreography traveling seamlessly from Hero through About, Skills Constellation, Flagships, Honors, Journey, and the Zenith.
- **Integrated Procedural Audio**: In-browser celestial soundscape synthesizer powered by Web Audio API (zero external assets required, ambient drone & crystalline hover chimes).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: [React 18](https://react.dev/) + [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
- **3D Engine**: [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) + [React Three Fiber (@react-three/fiber)](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/) + [@react-three/drei](https://drei.docs.pmnd.rs/)
- **Smooth Physics Scroll**: [Lenis](https://lenis.darkroom.engineering/)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) with custom glassmorphism and gold gradient utilities
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Bundler**: [Vite 6](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Hosting**: Optimized for [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/)
